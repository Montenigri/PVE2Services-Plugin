ℹ️ How the wiki sync works & why

How it works: a documentation page is rendered per machine/node/storage from editable templates and pushed to Wiki.js via its GraphQL API, organized under the base path (e.g. infrastructure/proxmox/vm/115).

Why: infrastructure documentation that maintains itself — names, specs, IPs and status stay current without anyone editing pages.

Idempotent: every page is tracked by a content hash. Re-running sync never rewrites unchanged pages; changed machines are updated in place. Templates are Jinja2 ({{ name }}, loops, filters) — the full tag reference is at the top of the Templates tab.
